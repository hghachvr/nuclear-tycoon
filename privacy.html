Privacy Policy for Nuclear Tycoon

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Introduction

Nuclear Tycoon ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

Information We Collect

1. Game Progress Data

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your current money and resources
  • Upgrades and research completed
  • Number of rebirths
  • Game statistics and achievements

This data is NOT transmitted to our servers. It remains on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

2. Advertising Data

Nuclear Tycoon uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. When you watch an ad, Google may collect:

  • Your advertising ID
  • General location (approximate)
  • Device type and operating system
  • Ad interaction data

This data is collected and used by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Crash Reports

If the app crashes, basic error information may be collected to help us improve stability. This does not include personal information.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name or email address
  • Your exact location
  • Your contacts or personal files
  • Your browsing history
  • Sensitive personal information

How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Services

Nuclear Tycoon uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. See Google's Privacy Policy
  • Capacitor: For native app functionality. See Capacitor's Privacy

Data Retention

Your game progress is stored indefinitely on your device until you choose to delete it. You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Reset all progress" option in the game settings.

Children's Privacy

Nuclear Tycoon is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your game data (stored on your device)
  • Delete your game data at any time
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
